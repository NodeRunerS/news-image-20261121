## 1. LEAD — Poland invokes Article 4 as Russian missiles land on NATO soil
- Two Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone fell in
  south-eastern Poland (Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem, Molodycz) during
  Russia's overnight 20 Nov attack on Ukraine — 400+ drones and 110+
  missiles, 27 ballistic. Warsaw called it an intentional provocation,
  timed to allied drills near Nowa Deba; Poland formally requested more
  NATO assets (AWACS, F-35s) under Operation Eastern Sentry and invoked
  Article 4. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called it a "grave
  violation". President Trump dismissed it as "by mistake".
- Sources: AP, Reuters, Kyiv Independent, Washington Post, 21 Nov.

## 2. COP31 runs into overtime with no deal as fossil fuels are cut (Climate)
- The Antalya summit missed its Friday close; a third draft was promised
  for Saturday and the final plenary slipped to Sunday. The revised draft
  (cut from 144 to 80 paragraphs) contains no mention of coal, oil or gas,
  and drops the proposed $120bn-a-year adaptation finance target for 2035.
  About 130 countries demanded the fossil-fuel language be restored; a
  large group marched on the presidency office on Saturday evening. The
  draft would be the first COP text to admit warming will pass 1.5°C.
  Next summit: Addis Ababa, November 2027.
- Sources: BBC, Carbon Brief, Asia Today, 21 Nov.

## 3. Trump says the next Iran move may wait until 2027 (Middle East)

  not come until 2027; Tehran wants a deal "not acceptable" to him. The
  US naval blockade around Hormuz was expanded to vessels taking cargo in
  ship-to-ship transfers from Iranian ships. Mossad warned Iran expanded

  of Jurf al-Sakhr under a Quds Force-brokered deal giving the militia

- Sources: AP, CBS, ISW-CTP, 21 Nov.

## 4. Nvidia board refuses to bring Huang back (Tech)
